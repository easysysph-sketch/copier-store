Privacy Policy

CopierStore collects information needed to create accounts, process orders, provide delivery and repair services, communicate with customers, and maintain store records.

Information we use

This may include your name, email, phone number, delivery addresses, order details, support messages, repair details and payment references or receipts you choose to submit.

How information is protected

Account passwords are stored using password hashing. Payment receipts and repair photos are kept in protected server-side storage rather than the public static directory.

Third parties

Optional delivery and AI features may send only the information required for the selected service. API credentials are kept server-side.

Your account

You can review or delete eligible account information from your customer settings. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate business, accounting or transaction purposes.
